# Scam Alert Workshops

**Status**: Proposed
**Lead**: Looking for lead
**Started**: —

## Problem Statement

Garner residents—especially seniors and less tech-savvy community members—are increasingly targeted by sophisticated scams. Phishing emails, robocalls, AI-generated voice clones, and social engineering attacks are causing real financial and emotional harm. Many people don't know how to recognize these threats or protect themselves.

There's no free, local, accessible education on this topic in Garner.

## Proposed Solution

A monthly workshop series hosted at the Garner branch of Wake County Public Library, teaching residents to recognize, avoid, and report scams. Each session focuses on a specific threat type with:

- Real-world examples (anonymized local stories when possible)
- Hands-on practice identifying red flags
- Take-home "cheat sheets" for quick reference
- Q&A and one-on-one help

### Workshop Topics

| Session | Topic | Description |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Phishing 101 | Spotting fake emails, texts, and websites |
| 2 | Phone Scams | IRS, Medicare, tech support, grandparent scams |
| 3 | AI-Powered Scams | Deepfake voices, fake videos, romance scams |
| 4 | Password Security | Strong passwords, password managers, 2FA setup |
| 5 | Social Media Privacy | What you're exposing, how to lock down accounts |
| 6 | Protecting Your Family | Parental controls, kids online, family conversations |

## Skills Needed

- **Security knowledge** — Understanding of common scam tactics and protective measures
- **Teaching/presenting** — Ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences
- **Content creation** — Slide decks, handouts, cheat sheets
- **Community outreach** — Promoting workshops, recruiting attendees
- **Spanish/other languages** (nice to have) — For multilingual sessions

## Time Commitment

- **Planning**: 3-5 hours per workshop (content creation, logistics)
- **Delivery**: 1-2 hours per workshop (including setup and Q&A)
- **Ongoing**: Monthly cadence, can rotate facilitators

## Impact

### Who Benefits
- Seniors vulnerable to phone and email scams
- Parents wanting to protect their families online
- Small business owners handling customer data
- Anyone who uses email, phones, or social media

### Expected Outcomes
- Reduced financial losses to scams in the community
- Increased adoption of 2FA and password managers
- More confident, security-aware residents
- Community connections between neighbors
