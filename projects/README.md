## 📋 Proposing a Project

To propose a new project:

1. Open an issue with the `project-idea` label
2. Include:
   - **Problem Statement**: What problem does this solve for Garner?
   - **Proposed Solution**: High-level approach
   - **Skills Needed**: What kind of help you're looking for
   - **Time Commitment**: Estimated effort
   - **Impact**: Who benefits and how?
