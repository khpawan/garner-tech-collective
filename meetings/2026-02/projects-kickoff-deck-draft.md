# Project 1
## Scam Alert Workshops

---

## Scam Alert Workshops — problem

Garner residents (especially seniors) are targeted by:
- phishing emails/texts
- phone scams (IRS/Medicare/tech support)
- AI-enabled scams (voice clones, deepfakes)

There’s no free, local, accessible education in Garner.

---

## Scam Alert Workshops — solution

A monthly workshop series at the Garner Library:

- real examples + red flags
- hands-on practice
- take-home cheat sheets
- Q&A + one-on-one help

---

## Scam Alert Workshops — MVP (6–8 weeks)

Milestone 1 (week 1–2)
- confirm library partner + date
- Session #1 outline (Phishing 101)
- 1-page handout draft

Milestone 2 (week 3–4)
- slide deck v1 + demo examples
- promo flyer + community posts
- run-through + refine

Roles needed
- Lead/facilitator
- Content helper
- Volunteer helper (Q&A)

# Project 2
## Town Council Summarizer

---

## Town Council Summarizer — problem

Town Council meetings affect:
- zoning
- budgets
- infrastructure
- local policy

But most residents don’t engage because meetings are long and hard to follow.

---

## Town Council Summarizer — solution

Pipeline:

1) capture meeting (YouTube) + agenda/minutes
2) transcribe (captions or Whisper)
3) summarize (structured, plain English)
4) human review (accuracy + context)
5) publish (newsletter + web archive)

---

## Town Council Summarizer — MVP (4–6 weeks)

Milestone 1 (week 1–2)
- pick one meeting video source
- produce transcript
- define summary template (sections + tone)

Milestone 2 (week 3–4)
- generate 1 summary with human review
- publish to a simple archive (GitHub Pages / Notion)
- share with 3–5 locals for feedback

Roles needed
- Lead/editor (quality + fact-check process)
- Engineer (pipeline)
- Writer/helper (plain-English clarity)
